6. CAMPUS SURVIVAL FAQS
================================================================================

Q: Is 75% attendance strictly mandatory?
A: Yes. 75% aggregate is enforced for mid-term and end-term exam admit cards.
   For genuine medical cases, submit medical certificates within 7 days of
   absence to the Academic Branch (Room 102).

Q: Where can we get cheap printouts on campus?
A: 1. Nescafe kiosk xerox shop behind MAE building (Rs 1/page).
   2. Central Library ground floor offers 20 free digital print pages every
      semester via your student roll number login.

Q: What is the hostel curfew and leave procedure?
A: Entry deadline is 9:00 PM on weekdays. For night outs or weekend leaves,
   submit an e-leave form signed by parents on the hostel portal 24 hours in
   advance, followed by parental SMS confirmation.
