## 6. Campus Survival FAQs

> **Q: Is the 75% attendance rule strictly enforced?**  
> **A:** Yes. An aggregate 75% attendance in theory and labs is mandatory to receive end-term exam admit cards. Genuine medical leaves must be submitted with official hospital slips to the Academic Branch (Room 102) within 7 days of absence.

> **Q: Where can students get photocopies and printing done on campus?**  
> **A:** 
> 1. The Nescafe kiosk print shop behind MAE building (₹1/page).  
> 2. The Central Library Ground Floor provides 20 free print pages per semester via your student roll number login.

> **Q: How can hostel residents apply for night out or late leave?**  
> **A:** Submit an e-leave form signed by parents via the hostel portal at least 24 hours in advance. Parent SMS verification is verified at the security gate turnstile.
